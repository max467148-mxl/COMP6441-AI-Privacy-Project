# Limitations

- The profiles are fictional and cannot prove real-world leakage rates.
- The experiment measures model behaviour under a specific prompt design.
- The automated scorer is a first-pass tool; final scoring should include manual review.
- Confidence values are self-reported by the model and may not be calibrated.
- The project studies privacy inference risk, not unauthorised database access.
- Results should be described as observations from this controlled experiment only.
